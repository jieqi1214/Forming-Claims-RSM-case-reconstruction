S–C Reconstruction Record

This repository accompanies Forming Claims in Evidence Synthesis: From Synthesis Findings to Review Conclusions. The S–C Reconstruction Record documents the relation between a selected material review conclusion and the completed synthesis finding(s) that support it, including any additional or disputed proposition, its point of entry, the methodological basis relevant to its appraisal, and the resulting conclusion-level action.

The record is a documentary output for making an application of the method inspectable and shareable.

The repository contains an editable one-page template and the reconstruction materials used in the paper's retrospective demonstrations.
